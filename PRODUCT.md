# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Retail and business banking customers in India — existing account holders looking for rates, login and service routes, and prospects comparing savings accounts, deposits, cards and loans. Source: home and personal/business banking hubs (`stardust/current/pages/*.json`).

## Product Purpose

The public website of RBL Bank: "one of India’s leading private sector banks, offering a range of personal and business banking products and services, including Savings and Current Accounts, Loans, Credit Cards, NRI Services, and Investments" (home meta description, verbatim).

## Positioning

"A Bank You Can Call Your ‘Own’" (about-us hero) — the brand line "apno ka bank" sits under the logo.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Drupal 10 (bootstrap_barrio theme) site with mega-menu header, product carousels, a Fixed Deposit calculator, embedded video carousel and an accessibility widget.
- Regulated financial content: rates, disclaimers and legal footer links must be carried verbatim.

## Brand Commitments

- Register: `brand` (marketing/landing hubs with product cross-sell).
- Personality observed: institutional, approachable, offer-led (rate call-outs, promotional banners).
- Anti-references observed: none stated.
