Frequently asked Questions

How can I find the nearest RBL Bank branch?

You can find the nearest RBL Bank branch by using the Branch Locator on the Contact Us page. Alternatively, use the RBL Cares Chatbot and select Banking Services → Branch Locator.

How do I contact RBL Bank for account-related queries?

You can contact RBL Bank Customer Care at +91 22 6232 7777 or use the Raise a Query / Request / Complaint feature on the website.

Can I contact RBL Bank for credit card support?

Yes, for credit card support including billing, payments, and activation, contact Customer Care at +91 22 6232 7777 or raise a request via the website.

How do I submit feedback or complaints to RBL Bank?

You can submit feedback or complaints using the Raise a Query / Request / Complaint feature on the website.

My RBL Bank account was closed without notification. What can I do?

Please contact Customer Care at +91 22 6232 7777 to understand the reason for the account closure.

What if RBL Bank Customer Care is not responding?

If Customer Care is not responding, submit your request using the online complaint form for quicker resolution.

I am being harassed by RBL Bank recovery agents. What should I do?

If you experience harassment from collection agents, report it using the online complaint form on the website.

How do I get my RBL Bank loan statement or resolve repayment issues?

For loan statements or repayment issues, contact Customer Care at +91 22 6232 7777 or submit your request via the website.

My RBL Bank transaction failed but money was deducted. What should I do?

In case of a failed transaction, the amount is usually reversed within 7–10 business days. If not, contact Customer Care at +91 22 6232 7777.
